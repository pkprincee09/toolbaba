Privacy Policy

This page explains how Toolbaba handles information when you visit and use our website and online tools.

Last updated: September 14, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Toolbaba. We provide simple online tools, calculators and other useful resources designed to help users complete everyday tasks more easily.

This Privacy Policy explains the general approach Toolbaba takes toward information and privacy when you use our website.

2. Information We Collect

Many Toolbaba tools are designed to work directly in your browser. Depending on the tool and how you use the website, information may include:

  • Information you voluntarily provide through contact forms.
  • Messages or feedback you send to us.
  • Basic technical information that may be provided by your browser or hosting services.
  • Usage information that may be collected through analytics or similar services, if enabled.
Toolbaba does not ask users to provide unnecessary sensitive personal information in order to use ordinary online calculators.

3. Information Entered Into Tools

Some calculators require you to enter numbers or other information to produce a result.

Where a tool performs calculations in your browser, the information entered may be processed locally by the webpage rather than being intentionally submitted to Toolbaba.

Users should avoid entering passwords, payment credentials, identity documents or other highly sensitive information into general-purpose tools.

4. Cookies

Toolbaba may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary for website functionality, analytics, security or advertising services.

Third-party advertising or analytics providers may use cookies according to their own policies.

5. Third-Party Services

Toolbaba may use third-party services for functions such as website hosting, analytics, advertising, performance monitoring or communication.

These third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Advertising

Toolbaba may display advertisements in the future. Advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure or personalize advertisements where permitted.

Users should review the applicable policies of the advertising providers for more information about their data practices.

7. External Links

Toolbaba may contain links to external websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of websites operated by third parties.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any external website you visit.

8. Children's Privacy

Toolbaba is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly request personal information from children in situations where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to maintain the security of information associated with our website. However, no internet transmission or online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as our website, tools or services change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.
